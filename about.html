Biography

I am an Urban Planner, BIM Architect, and Transportation Specialist based in Scarborough, Ontario. As a Candidate Member of the Ontario Professional Planners Institute (OPPI) working toward full RPP/MCIP credentials, my practice focuses on converting complex municipal growth frameworks, zoning bylaws, and structural constraints into actionable 3D urban developments.

My methodology pairs sustainable transportation planning and land-use policy with technical BIM execution. Backed by postgraduate specializations from Seneca Polytechnic and Fanshawe College, I lead technical studies encompassing Major Transit Station Area (MTSA) master planning, Transit-Oriented Development (TOD), active transportation networks, and multi-trade BIM coordination.

From automating parametric workflows using Autodesk Revit and Dynamo to conducting rigorous Traffic Impact Assessments (TIA), I bridge the divide between high-level urban policy and precise technical project implementation.

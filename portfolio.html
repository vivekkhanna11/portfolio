Rebalancing Mobility at Bridletowne Circle

Location: Scarborough, Toronto, Warden Ave & Finch Ave E

A close look at how a high-density residential node gets stranded inside a loop-road suburban plan built for cars. The study traces the conflict between transit-dependent residents and an arterial designed purely for vehicle throughput, then builds a phased path toward a fine-grain, mixed-use block.

Crossing width~36 m
Posted speed60 km/h
Cross-section6 lanes
Refuge islandsNone
Key deliverables
  • Network, transit, and safety audit across three infrastructure layers
  • Policy alignment scorecard against Vision Zero, the Official Plan, and the Cycling Network Plan
  • Short, medium, and long-term intervention roadmap
  • Before and after concept: superblock retrofit to a fine-grain, active-frontage plan

Plate 02, Community History N ↑
PLATE 02 / 04

Forest Hill South, History & Theories of Community Planning

Location: Forest Hill South, Toronto, Eglinton W to St. Clair W, between Bathurst and Spadina/Avenue Rd

A century-long look at a Garden City era village that was incorporated as its own municipality in 1924, long before it was folded back into Toronto. The study traces lot pattern, land use, transportation, and institutional form from 1939 to today, then measures the neighbourhood's founding values against the Seven Grandfather Teachings.

Incorporated1924
Population~10,860
Detached / semi~70%
Study span1939 to 2024
Key deliverables
  • Demographic and housing profile benchmarked against City of Toronto data
  • Four-element built-form analysis: lot configuration, land use, transportation, institutions
  • Aerial and map comparison from 1939 to 2024, with findings on what drove the changes
  • Thirty-year vision that reframes the original planning values through Indigenous teachings
